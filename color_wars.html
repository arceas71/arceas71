    How to play

    Each cell holds a critical mass — 2 orbs in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 in the center.

    Tap an empty cell or one of your own color to add an orb. Reach critical mass and the cell explodes, firing orbs into every neighbor and converting them to your color — often setting off a chain reaction.

    You can only play in empty cells or cells you already own. A player is eliminated once they hold zero orbs after their first turn. Last color standing wins.
